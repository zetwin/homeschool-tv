### The three ideas we built
| Prototype | Idea | References |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Shelves** | One big featured pack on top, then rows by kind, sliding sideways | App Store Today, Apple Books Reading Now, Netflix rows, Khan Kids Library |
| **B. This week's bag** | The week's pages first, ready to print or send to devices, children as small marks. A shelf of kinds below to add more | Epic Assign and Mailbox, Kumon Connect daily delivery, Workybooks assign, Apple Books Want to Read |
| **C. Subjects** | Big subject pictures; tap one to see its pages as large covers with a time and "print or device" tags; tap a page to see it full size | Khan Kids Library tabs, Teachers Pay Teachers cover then preview, BrainPOP labelled cards, Apple Books Library |

The research suggested a split view for C, but the owner dropped split views in pad round 2 (the team's private taste notes), so C opens pages full screen instead. A path, as in Duolingo or ABCmouse, suits daily practice better than a library: it appears inside B as a step strip for maths.
