## England's national curriculum
Whole document: `channel/official/england-national-curriculum.pdf`, 264 pages. In the app: the parent pad, then The full program.

- **Source:** [gov.uk framework for key stages 1 to 4](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-framework-for-key-stages-1-to-4). Open Government Licence v3.0.
- **Is it current?** It's the version in force. The page was last updated on 2 December 2014. England has [committed to a revised curriculum](https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/id/eprint/41839/1/SN06798.pdf), to be published in 2027 for first teaching in 2028. Recheck this file then.
- **What it is:** goals for each stage, not a lesson list. Years 5 and 6 are key stage 2, and Years 7 to 9 are key stage 3. Our lesson order comes from Oak National Academy, a separate free program.
- **Not in it:** Relationships and Health Education, which is [separate statutory guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/relationships-education-relationships-and-sex-education-rse-and-health-education). Emotional Intelligence follows that one.
