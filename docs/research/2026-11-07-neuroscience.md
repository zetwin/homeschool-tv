# Neuroscience: where it fits, and Lesson 1's review notes

Date: 2026-11-07. Owner asked: "we need Neorol science please, is it category subject? where it should be placed in program?"

## The answer
**Neuroscience is a topic, not a separate school subject at this age.** It belongs under **Science → Biology → The human body**, right after Human skeleton and muscles, because nerves are what tell muscles when to pull.

It's a **bonus unit**: "Your brain and nerves". It's in the program, but doesn't fill timetable classes, because it goes beyond the official objectives for ages 10 to 12.

The first lesson follows Oak's outcome, simplified: describe "the parts that make up the human nervous system that enables us to sense and respond to changes".

**Other countries weren't checked.** The owner hasn't chosen a launch country yet.

## The unit: 6 lessons, in Oak's order, simplified
1. How Your Brain Talks to Your Body (built)
2. Neurons up close
3. How fast can you react? (Oak's practical)
4. Reflexes in detail
5. Inside your brain
6. Looking after your brain

The eye lessons are left out because Year 7 already has Sound, light and vision.
