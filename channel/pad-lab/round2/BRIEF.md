## What changes in round 2
1. **Organised by child, with shapes, not paragraphs.** Cards, tiles, rings and ticks. Few words, short labels. No time grids, no newspaper, no chat.
2. **Marking and homework move one tap deeper.** Tests mark themselves. Nothing on the first screen says "Give a mark" or "Set homework". They live inside a class or a child, one tap away. The only marking on the first screen is a small badge when written exam answers wait for a parent ("3 answers to mark").
3. **Homework is for everyone by default.** When a parent adds homework, it goes to all children, with a choice to pick one child.
4. **Each version must be clearly better or clearly different from its round 1 parent.** Say in one sentence what it tests.
5. Admin stays its own full page at `#admin`. Fit 1180x820 and 820x1180 with no page scroll, at normal and Large text. Text >= 17px, contrast >= 4.5:1, targets >= 44px.
