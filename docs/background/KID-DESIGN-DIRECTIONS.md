# Kid design directions

Last updated: 2026-11-06. **Status: the owner rejected the quality of all ten ("poor, weak, like kid made") and kept D, F and G as the right direction. A new round needs real art and materials. See `docs/research/2026-11-06-kid-design-polish.md`. None of this is in the real app.**

See them on the home server, at `/kid-lab/`. Every direction shows the same child, lesson, classes and homework, so only the design differs. Each has a Home, Test question and Homework screen, at iPad and phone size.

## The problem
The owner said the child's screens look poor and weak. They look like a plain office dashboard: same cards, same sizes, same polite type. Changing colors won't fix that. A design has to differ in structure.

## What can change
| Ingredient | The range we can use |
|---|---|
| **Layout** | Three giant blocks, a poster, a focused row, a mosaic, a landscape, objects on a desk, a side rail, a clock, a fan of cards |
| **Navigation** | Top text, side rail, hotbar, paper index tabs, pills, or no menu on the home at all |
| **Type** | Huge rounded, tall condensed capitals, clean geometric, handwriting, pixel, strict with fixed-width times, chunky display |
| **Color and accent** | Primary color blocks, black and one color, the lesson photo as the color, soft tints, sky and grass, paper and a desk, stone and grass, off-white and one strong blue, deep blue with bright cards |
| **Wording** | One word (“Learn”), punchy (“Start now”), almost none (“Play”), warm (“Let’s go”), a journey (“You are here”, “In my backpack”), a note to yourself (“Don’t forget”), plain (“Begin”) |
| **Tile size and shape** | Screen-filling, mixed sizes, tall cards, round stops, square slots, one big plus small ones, or no tiles at all |
| **How things are separated** | Color alone, depth and light, thin lines, the scene itself, paper edges, raised and sunken edges |
| **Pictures** | Full-screen photo, a photo in a shape, a taped print, a card picture, 3D icons as stickers |
| **How much shows** | One decision only, up to everything at once |
| **Motion and sound** (not in the lab yet) | Tiles that pop, a path that draws itself, paper that flips, a soft click |

## The ten directions
| | Name | The idea | Best for | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Big Three** | Three giant doors, nothing else | No decisions to make | Today’s classes sit behind a tap |
| B | **Poster** | A magazine cover, with today’s lesson as the headline | Feeling older and cool | Needs a strong photo for every lesson |
| C | **Console** | A big focused tile on the lesson’s own photo | Feeling premium, and it can also work on the TV | Dark, and To do is smaller |
| D | **Bento** | A mosaic of very different tile sizes | Seeing everything at once | Busy, and closest to a normal app |
| E | **Day Path** | The day as a journey, each class a stop | Order and progress at a glance | Can feel young, and must stay free of points |
| F | **Desk** | A real diary with a taped photo and sticky notes | The owner’s щоденник idea, warm and unusual | Handwriting is harder to read |
| G | **Block Hub** | Stone panels, class slots, a hotbar | Children who love block-building games | A strong style can tire, and pixel type limits reading |
| H | **Studio** | A side rail with big picture rows | Children who love game platforms, and easy to grow | Closest to what we have now |
| I | **Time Rail** | The day as a clock, strict and grown-up | Older children, trust, and exams | The least fun |
| J | **Deck** | Every class is a big card, the next one held up | Delight, and real achievement cards later | Fewer things fit |

All ten use original drawings and type. None copies a real game’s art or name, and none has points, coins or streaks.

## Mixing
These can be combined. For example, a child could choose a direction as their look, the way themes already work, or we could keep one structure and offer others as skins. Structure costs far more to offer as a choice than color does, because each one needs its own layouts for every screen.

## Owner's choice
**D Bento, F Desk and G Block Hub stay.** The rest are dropped from the lab.
