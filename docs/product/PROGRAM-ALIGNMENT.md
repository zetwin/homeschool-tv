## What this means
- **Science is the best matched.** It follows England's national curriculum and Oak's lesson order.
- **Geography, Emotional Intelligence and Neuroscience are honest extras.** They're aligned to official goals, but the series are our own.
- **Spanish follows Oak, but the children are past it.** A new plan for writing and speaking is needed.
- **Which country's program the family follows is still open** (`ROADMAP.md`). Until it's decided, England's program is the reference.
- **Not yet done:** an expert teacher checking any lesson. The fact-checker helper is an AI, not a teacher.
