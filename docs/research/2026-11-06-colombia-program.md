# Colombia in school programs

Date: 2026-11-06. Some Britannica, StatPearls and California details came from search extracts. See "Not verified".

## Answer
**Mainly geography, ending with two history lessons.** Best spot: ages 9 to 11, after an Americas unit. In our program it's a family unit, not an Oak unit.

## How real programs place it
**England** ([geography](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-geography-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-geography-programmes-of-study), [history](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-history-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-history-programmes-of-study))
- KS2 geography: locate countries using maps focused on Europe and North and South America, and compare a UK region with a region in a European country and a region in North or South America.
- KS3 geography focuses on Africa, Russia, Asia and the Middle East. The Americas aren't named.
- KS2 history: one study from early Islamic civilization, Maya or Benin. The Muisca aren't on the list, so they're an extra.
- Oak: [Year 5 Americas](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/curriculum/geography-primary/units/north-and-south-america-how-diverse-are-their-places-and-landscapes), [Year 5 Amazon](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/curriculum/geography-primary/units/south-america-why-does-the-amazon-matter), [Year 6 Maya](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/curriculum/history-primary/units/non-european-societies-benin-or-the-maya-1701). No Colombia unit found.

**Colombia** ([Learning Rights](https://colombiaaprende.edu.co/sites/default/files/files_public/2026-05/dba_c.sociales-v2_0.pdf), [Standards](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/1621/articles-340021_recurso_1.pdf))
- Grade 4: Indigenous peoples such as Muisca, Tairona, Calima and Zenú.
- Grade 5: regions, two oceans, thermal floors, colonial New Granada, independence.
- Grade 6: pre-Hispanic legacies. The Standards put natural regions in grades 6–7, so the two documents differ slightly.
- Armed conflict and drug trafficking appear in grades 9–10.

**Elsewhere:** California grade 7 covers [Meso-American and Andean civilizations](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/id/web/4369). Texas grade 6 [names South America](https://tea.texas.gov/sites/default/files/113b_11-16-2018.pdf). [Spain's national decree](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2022/03/02/pdfs/BOE-A-2022-3296.pdf) doesn't mention America; regional programs weren't checked.

## The eight-lesson series
The owner found six lessons too thin: no people, culture or food. Expanded on 2026-11-06.
| # | Subject | Lesson | Follows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Geography | One country, six regions. **Built** | KS2 location. The six regions are extra detail |
| 2 | Geography | Climbing the climate | KS2 climate zones, Colombia grade 5 |

| 4 | Geography | Coffee and food: growing coffee, arepas and regional dishes, comparison with home | KS2 region comparison: the strongest fit |

| 7 | History | The golden raft: evidence and legend | Extra in England. Colombia grade 4 |
| 8 | History | A new nation | Extra at KS2, arguable at KS3. Colombia grade 5 |

## How to treat Colombia
- Leave recent armed conflict and drugs out at this age, which matches Colombia's own program. Prepare one calm, honest answer for children who ask.
- My judgment: no drug jokes, show modern cities, describe Indigenous peoples in the present tense, avoid the word "discovered", and be honest about the conquest.
- Warning against imagining distant places as backward: [Scoffham](https://freedomtoteach.collins.co.uk/?p=877).
- **Ask a Colombian to review it.**

## Facts and safe wording
Sourced in the fact-check notes for Lesson 1:
- Two coasts: [ANH](https://www.anh.gov.co/documents/12/colombian_sedimentary_basins.pdf).
- Population: [DANE projection](https://www.dane.gov.co/index.php/estadisticas-por-tema/demografia-y-poblacion/proyecciones-de-poblacion), 53,399,171 for 2026.
- Birds: [Guinness/BirdLife](https://guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/684249-most-bird-species-country), 1,866, ahead of Peru. Say "more than 1,800, the most in recent counts".
- Wax palm: national tree by law. Can reach [over 60 m](https://jbb.gov.co/historias-del-verde-urbano-el-simbolo-patrio-arboreo-de-colombia-llego-a-camino-verde/) by one source, usually 45 m elsewhere. Say "one of the tallest palms".
- Caño Cristales: [National Parks](https://www.parquesnacionales.gov.co/sala-prensa/noticias/inicia-temporada-en-cano-cristales-naturalmente-responsable/): a water plant, not algae.
- Thermal floors: bands vary by hundreds of meters. Say "about".
- Highest peak: recent measurements are disputed ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14978)). Say "twin peaks over 5,700 m".
- Six regions: lists differ. Say "Colombians often divide their country into six regions".
