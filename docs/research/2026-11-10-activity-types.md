## Game parts in real products
- **Collections:**
  - Khan Kids gives items for characters as topics are mastered. [Khan Kids](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049358751)
  - Prodigy's pets grow. Reviewers call its paid extras pushy. [Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/prodigy-math-game-what-parents-need-to-know)
- **Mystery reveal:** a picture fills in, or locks open one by one.
- **Forgiving streaks:**
  - Brilliant's "streak charges" cover a missed day. [Brilliant](https://help.brilliant.org/en/articles/8507079-streak-charge-faq)
  - Duolingo counts a short review toward the streak. [Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/tips-for-maintaining-streak)
- **Warnings:**
  - Weekly leagues push low scorers down. Studies find they lose motivation, and a shared goal is suggested instead. [NSF](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10510520), [EdWeek](https://www.edweek.org/education/opinion-to-leaderboard-or-not-the-art-of-motivating-and-monitoring-performance/2016/03)
  - Streak anxiety: opinion only, no study. [Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/duolingo-streaks-and-anxiety-in-kids)

## Paper-saving tricks
- **Cards:**
  - Quarter-page cards, 4 per sheet, laminated and reused.
  - Dry-erase on a plastic sleeve.
  - One answer sheet for many cards. [Guide](https://climbtheladder.com/what-are-task-cards-and-how-do-they-work/)
- **Printing:**
  - Print 2 pages per sheet, or at A5. [Tes](https://www.tes.com/teaching-resource/spanish-greetings-12896127)
  - Reading cards on card stock.
  - Answer keys kept apart.
  - Printer-friendly versions without heavy borders.
- **QR codes to check answers.** QR booklets must be single-sided. [Example](https://www.superteacherworksheets.com/printable-pdf-worksheets/division/28139-QR-Code-Divide-&-Check)
- **Double-sided printing as a habit:** **UNVERIFIED**.

## Names we must not use
- **Trademarks:**
  - "Doodle Notes": say "half-drawn notes".
  - KenKen and KenDoku: say "number cage puzzle".
  - Mad Libs: say "funny fill-in".
  - Boom Cards (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Other names to avoid:**
  - Breakout EDU: say "escape challenge".
  - Jeopardy (**UNVERIFIED**).
  - "Weird But True" (a Nat Geo series name).
  - Game-mode names such as Gold Quest or Karate Cats.
  - Wordwall's own template names. Plain words like "match up" are fine.
