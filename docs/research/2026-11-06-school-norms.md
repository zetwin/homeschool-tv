# School timetable norms

Date: 2026-11-06. From search extracts unless marked. Not read in full. Check the original before quoting these to a customer.

## England
- Non-statutory expectation of a 32.5-hour school week, including breaks: [DfE guidance](https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/id/eprint/40013/1/Minimum_School_Week_Non-Statutory_Guidance.pdf). Most recent copy found is from 2023.
- Council-maintained schools open at least 190 days a year: [House of Commons Library briefing](https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/id/eprint/40093/1/SN07148.pdf). Academies set their own.
- No national table of hours per subject was found. These are individual schools only:
  - [Shrewsbury Academy](https://shrewsburyacademy.co.uk/?p=4640): English 4, Maths 4, Science 3, of 25 hours a week.
  - [St Thomas More, Buxton](https://www.stthomasmorebuxton.srscmat.co.uk/?p=4082), [Hatch End](https://www.hatchend.harrow.sch.uk/336/time-allocation): similar, some by fortnight.
- **Home education:** parents aren't required to have a timetable, set hours, or follow school hours, days or terms: [DfE guide for parents](https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/id/eprint/31441/3/Elective%20home%20educationGuide%20for%20parents.pdf), an older copy. Records may be in any form: [Luton](https://www.luton.gov.uk/schools-education/elective-home-education-ehe/planning-your-childs-home-education).

## Ukraine
- 5-day week: grade 5, 28 hours; grade 6, 31; grade 7, 32; grades 8 and 9, 33: [osvita.ua](https://osvita.ua/school/80698/). May not reflect the [2024 update](https://mon.gov.ua/news/vidpovidaiemo-na-zapytannia-shchodo-onovlenoi-typovoi-osvitnoi-prohramy-dlia-59-klasiv-nush).
- **Not verified by a source:** the search found nothing on the official format of a щоденник, 45-minute lessons, or the 12-point scale. These come from the owner's memory and general knowledge. Ask a Ukrainian teacher to check.

## Homework
- Secondary homework linked to about five months' progress, moderate evidence: [summary of EEF](https://structural-learning.com/post/homework-evidence-based-policy-guide).
- Short and focused work is advised over large amounts: same summary. No EEF minutes-per-day figure was found.
