# What a parent's screen is for

Date: 2026-11-06. Written after the owner rejected the six parent looks: "no clear point", "looks like kid made them". **Mostly my judgment from what I saw. The owner is a real parent, and corrects this.**

## How this was done, and its limits
- Web searches, plus looking at real sites and Dribbble searches in a hidden browser.
- **No research on homeschool parents' real needs turned up.** Search results were app listings, which show what apps advertise, not what parents say.
- "Premium" articles were agency and tool blogs, not studies. None analyzed Things, Linear or Oura directly.
- Dribbble shows concept art. Much was never built, and I saw it small.
- I couldn't see inside Things, Linear, Oura, Seesaw or ClassDojo as a user, only their public pages.

## Why the last six failed
1. **Started from layouts, not a parent's questions.** So a grid of empty dashes took up the most space.
2. **Same parts in every look:** rounded cards, colored left edges, pills and the same list. Only the paint changed.
3. **The day didn't make sense:** a lesson "now" while children sat a different test.
4. **No hierarchy:** everything was medium-sized, so nothing said "start here".
5. **Enlarging was added afterward,** rather than designed in.

## Added later the same day: what parents find hard
Read from the original pages, after the second six were rejected. Both are small, so these are hints only.
- [Medlin, Home School Researcher, 1995](https://nheri.org/home-school-researcher-home-schooling-whats-hard-what-helps/): 163 of 975 Florida convention parents replied, in 1994 (17%, not a fair sample of families). Hardest: time with a spouse and alone (3.51 of 5), interruptions during school time (3.32), physical exhaustion (3.16). Most helpful: belief that home schooling is best (4.63), spiritual encouragement (4.60), other home schoolers (4.42), a supportive family (4.41).
- [Leech and others, Home School Researcher, posted 2025](https://nheri.org/schooling-during-covid-19-homeschooling-parents-stress-levels-measured-by-the-professional-quality-of-life-scale-proqol-survey/): 64 parents, self-reported, in September 2020. Experienced homeschoolers showed less burnout than new ones (19.35 and 26.48). It doesn't say which supports helped.
- What products offer: [Time4Learning](https://www.time4learning.com/online-homeschool), [Homeschool Planet](https://homeschoolplanet.com), [BJU Press hub](https://www.bjupresshomeschool.com/homeschool-hub), [Khan Academy's redesign](https://edtechinnovationhub.com/news/khan-academy-redesigns-classroom-platform-as-ai-tools-move-further-into-daily-teaching). These are descriptions and adverts, not evidence of use.
- **Still missing:** real parents' words. Forum searches returned only app listings. Asking a few home-schooling parents would beat any of this.
